Field guide

How to play

A fully interactive 3×3×3 cube with a built-in two-phase solver. Turn it yourself, scramble it, or let CubeLab find the path home.

Direct manipulation

Turn a layerDrag any sticker in the direction you want that row or column to move.
Orbit the viewClick and drag empty space around the cube to rotate your camera 360°.
ZoomScroll or pinch to move closer to the cube.
Middle slicesDrag the middle row of a face to turn the M, E, or S slice.

The solver

Kociemba two-phase reduction CubeLab computes a solution from the current state — usually 20 moves or fewer — then plays it back at your chosen speed. Slow, Medium, and Fast are in the playback bar above the controls.
Built with Three.js and cube.js (MIT). No accounts, no tracking, no network requests after the libraries load.
